Privacy policy

Collab Board is a portfolio project by Ahmed Gamal. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, when you sign up or sign in with Google or GitHub.

How it is used

Only to run the app: signing you in, showing your name and avatar to people you collaborate with, and recording board activity. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or tracking.
